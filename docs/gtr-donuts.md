# Nissan GT-R donuts

An original stylized R35 GT-R continuously drifts around a small loop near spawn. Use the **GT-R donuts** map marker to find it. `packages/shared/src/npc-layout.json` configures its center (-173.91, 8.12, 21.68), 4.6 m radius, 4.8 s lap and 0.8 rad drift angle. The path leaves spawn clear and was sampled for dry ground/building clearance.

`packages/shared/src/gtrDonuts.ts` defines its shared-clock pose, so clients see the same lap. The car has no boarding. The server samples the same pose and reuses traffic’s oriented car hitbox to knock down pedestrians and eject exposed bicycle/scooter riders. Impacts follow its actual travel direction, respect recovery protection and the No car collisions setting, and use the existing multiplayer impulse/effects messages. `apps/client/src/multiplayer/vehicles/gtr/GtrDonuts.ts` loads the model, grounds the body on actual road geometry (terrain fallback), counter-steers the front wheels, spins the rear wheels, renders two grounded circular tire marks and a bounded pool of 64 instanced smoke puffs. It uses MapGame's existing loop. Donut audio layers the game's recorded car-engine loop at a higher playback rate with an original procedural tire-squeal loop. Both fade over 50 m, respect vehicle sound settings and pause, and disappear with the source.

The model is original Blender geometry: red wide-body coupe, smoked windows, rear wing, quad circular lamps, quad exhaust, grille and separate wheel pivots. Editable source: `assets/blender/gtr.blend`; rebuild with `assets/blender/build-gtr.py`. Static parts are joined by material, and wheels retain their pivots. No third-party model is embedded.

Validation: periodic path/clearance, model footprint/wheel positions, slope grounding and lifecycle tests in `tests/gtr-donuts.test.ts`; browser check verifies actual motion, smoke, engine source and disposal.
