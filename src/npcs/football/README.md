# Tim Gio football corner

`createFootballStand(scene, world, {camera, placement, uiRoot, assetUrl, onClose, onReward, avoid})` builds the TV stand and stylised NPC. Placement remains map-owned (`position: [x,z]`, optional `heading`, or `searchAnchors`). Route `interact(position)` and `keydown(event)` through the host coordinator. Call `update(dt, {listener: playerPosition, muted, paused, enabled})` each frame, including when the player is walking elsewhere.

The full audio is split into eight contiguous goal sections (7–30 seconds each), covering all 125.386 seconds of the user-provided recording. They cycle in match order (Germany 1, Curaçao equaliser, Germany 2–7), with 7–12 second gaps by default. They start after a user gesture, play one at a time, pause out of range, and use player-distance falloff rather than camera distance. Maximum voice level is 0.38; near/far distances and gaps live in `game.config.json` under `npcs.football`. Muting or pausing stops audible playback. Talking pauses ambient playback. The two choices are “Hol ihn raus” and “Go away”; only the former plays the response and calls `onReward('tim-gio-football')` once per reset. Closing restores the camera. `reset` and idempotent `dispose` stop audio and release owned resources.

`createFootballItem(scene, world, {camera, inventory, canUse, onEquip, onHit, onKick})` uses the spare inventory slot. `add()` grants one reusable ball; repeated grants return false. 4 toggles it, Q/click kicks, and the ball leaves the inventory when kicked. It stays visible in the world after settling. Walk within 1.4 m and press E to pick it up; toggling the item never recalls it. Host calls `update(dt, playerPosition, {visible, walking, paused})`, routes `keydown`, calls `kick()` for pointer input, and routes world interaction to `pickup(playerPosition)`. `near(playerPosition)` reports a settled ball within pickup range. `onHit(from,to,velocity)` accepts a swept hit and should return whether a target was hit; `onKick({origin,velocity})` allows the destination host to send a multiplayer command. The local game connects hits to existing NPC knockdowns. Remote player replication and server hit validation belong to the destination multiplayer game.

The old penalty minigame has been removed. The TV remains a procedural match broadcast, and the model keeps the dark hoodie, headphones and Germany garland from the supplied reference. Original audio excerpt sources and timestamps are in `public/audio/CREDITS.md`.

Cut points and complete source coverage are recorded in `assets/audio/timgio-goal-edit.json`. Rebuild the audio with `python3 scripts/edit-timgio-audio.py /path/to/v26044gc0000d8nfo7fog65snbqicql0.MP4`. The script validates source duration and rejects gaps between sections. Natural pauses inside each goal reaction are preserved; only 15 ms edge fades and loudness normalization are applied.
