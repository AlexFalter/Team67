Einstein's teleporter has one charge per run. It randomly chooses one of three fixed stops: Bahnhofbrücke (-187, 50), Central (-23, 66), or lower Leonhardstrasse (23, -74). It preserves the GTA camera transition. There are no ETH/Polyterrasse destinations, and the player cannot select the most advantageous stop. Restarting restores the charge; returning to a spent machine opens depleted-capacitor feedback and disables the travel button.

Each stop is checked against the current map for boundary clearance, room around the landing, a walkable exit, a maximum 14 m height gain from the machine and a minimum 180 m distance from the finish. Unsafe stops are excluded rather than replaced with arbitrary random coordinates. Failed travel callbacks do not spend the charge.

Validation: `verify-einstein.mjs` covers dialogue, normal-mode GTA travel, one-use enforcement, depleted feedback and reset. `verify-einstein-bounds.mjs` checks all three fixed stops and actual landings, including outside-boundary rejection.

Einstein, the cabin kid and the food enthusiast share the RPG dialogue skin in `src/shared/dialogue/rpg-dialogue.css` and adapters/factory in `src/shared/dialogue/rpg-dialogue.js`. Einstein uses the factory with a named speaker, shared dialogue typography, selectable answers, status feedback and arrow/Enter/Escape controls. Existing cabin encounter logic adopts the same skin through `styleRPGDialogue`. Verification covers matching computed panel styles, mobile bounds, keyboard answers, teleport charge behavior, and direct kid/eater dialogue interaction. Older cabin walk-up regression scripts failed before reaching their dialogue panels during this check; direct dialogue checks passed.
