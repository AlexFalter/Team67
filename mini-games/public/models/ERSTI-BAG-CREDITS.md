ETH / VSETH Ersti-Bag

Original low-poly navy drawstring bag and first-person Pickup / Throw animation clips authored in Blender for this prototype. Reference: ETH Zürich D-ITET, Welcome Day 2020, photograph captioned 'Good stuff and helpful information inside the VSETH "Ersti" bags':
https://ee.ethz.ch/news-and-events/d-itet-news-channel/2020/09/welcome-to-d-itet.html

The reference photograph is not distributed as a game texture. White VSETH-style marking, cream drawstrings, gathered canvas and seams are modeled; lettering is adapted for readability. This is a recreation of the photographed 2020 bag, not a claim about the current year's exact design.

Source model: assets/blender/ersti-bag.blend
Rebuild script: assets/blender/build-ersti-bag.py
