# Black Ops III medal artwork

Source: [0taku's MP - Medals BO3 texture pack](https://forum.plutonium.pw/topic/12522/mp-medals-bo3), `medals vs final.rar`, linked at https://www.mediafire.com/file/yhddghz2k0ibm7s/medals_vs_final.rar/file . The pack provides BO3 artwork converted for BO2 modding. Original artwork belongs to Treyarch/Activision; no open redistribution licence is claimed.

The selected 128×128 IWI DXT1 textures were decoded to transparent PNG without repainting or stretching:

The humorous local medal titles and achievement conditions are game-specific adaptations. These are community-converted textures, not a newly verified extraction from a retail installation.
